##### 🗓️ Events
We actively participate in **events worldwide** and announce our presence on social networks, especially **LinkedIn**, so follow us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microcks) to **stay connected** with the Microcks [community](/community/). If you're an event organizer or [CNCF ambassador](https://www.cncf.io/people/ambassadors/program-standards/), feel free to invite us for talks, demos, or workshops—we’re passionate about learning and sharing!
